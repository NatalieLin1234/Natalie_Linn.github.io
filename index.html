As I continue my education, I want to understand how financial decisions shape a company’s future. My career goal is to become a financial analyst and help businesses make informed decisions about their spending, investments, and long-term plans. One reason I am drawn to finance is that it connects numbers to real situations. A company’s financial information can explain how it earns money, what challenges it faces, and where it might have opportunities to grow. I want to learn how to interpret that information and understand the reasons behind changes in performance. Being able to explain what the numbers mean is a skill I hope to develop throughout college and my future career. I am also interested in personal money management. I am especially interested in understanding the relationship between revenue, expenses, and profit. I also want to learn how businesses prepare budgets, evaluate results, and adjust their plans when circumstances change. Developing these skills will help me connect classroom concepts to practical business situations. Becoming a financial analyst appeals to me because the role involves research, analysis, and problem-solving. I would like to compare financial results, identify patterns, and help explain why a business is performing a certain way. I am also interested in learning how analysts create forecasts and evaluate possible investments. The opportunity to investigate questions and turn financial information into useful recommendations makes this career exciting to me. As I work toward this goal, I want to become more comfortable using Excel and other tools to organize and analyze information. I also hope to improve my communication skills so I can explain financial ideas clearly. Accurate calculations are valuable, but people also need to understand how those calculations relate to their decisions. I would like to gain experience through internships, projects, and opportunities to learn from professionals in the field. I am still exploring the different paths available within business and finance, and I look forward to discovering which areas interest me most. For now, my focus is on building a strong academic foundation and staying open to new experiences. I hope to develop the knowledge, judgment, and confidence needed to become a capable financial analyst.
